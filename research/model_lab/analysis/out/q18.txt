[18.1] Size of the invalid-density artifact
  A pmf on the .5 lattice obeys  q(y) = F(y+0.25) - F(y-0.25) <= 1.
  A continuous density at a point obeys nothing, and for a narrow
  scale exceeds 1 -- which is illegal as a predictive pmf.
    sigma= 0.25  max continuous density =   1.5958  (must be <= 1)
    sigma= 0.50  max continuous density =   0.7979  (must be <= 1)
    sigma= 1.00  max continuous density =   0.3989  (must be <= 1)
    sigma= 2.00  max continuous density =   0.1995  (must be <= 1)
    sigma=10.00  max continuous density =   0.0399  (must be <= 1)
  So at the learned sigma1 ~ 1.0 the continuous form is legal, but
  the SCALE ITSELF was mis-estimated because the illegal scores fed
  back into the online recursions.  Fixing the pmf changed the
  estimate, and the model got worse -- which is the point: the -0.818
  was never a real gain.
