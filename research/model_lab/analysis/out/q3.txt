[3.2] Is the GARCH scale simply mis-specified? (unit check)
  A correct scale recursion in observable units:
     s2_t = (1-a)*s2_{t-1} + a*x_t^2          <- EWMA, stable, this is D0
     s2_t = (1-a-b)*s2_{t-1} + a*x_t^2 + b*s2_{t-1}   <- GARCH, needs 2 knobs
  The D3 recursion standardised the shock first, so its accumulator is
  in units of 1/sigma^2 and compounds without bound.  Test:
     lam_g=0.90  median sd=     6.19  p99 sd=       16.08  mean NLL=3.6752
     lam_g=0.95  median sd=     6.31  p99 sd=       12.72  mean NLL=3.6638
     lam_g=0.97  median sd=     6.41  p99 sd=       11.85  mean NLL=3.6524
     lam_g=0.99  median sd=     6.63  p99 sd=       10.85  mean NLL=3.6247
