[4.2] Decompose ACF(z) into the two mechanisms that create it.
  ACF1(z) = 2*Cov(z_t,z_{t-1}) because sd(z)=1.
  raw x:      ACF1 = -0.2180

  1/sd alone: ACF1 = +0.9861   <- the ARTEFACT, no market content
  sd alone:   ACF1 = +0.9990

  => the sign flip is largely produced by dividing by a smooth sd.
